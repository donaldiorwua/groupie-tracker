# **Groupie Tracker**
**Groupie Tracker** is a music concert and events tracking platform that helps you discover artists and explore their upcoming and past performances. View detailed information about each artist, including their members, concert dates, venues, and performance locations.

Search for your favorite artist and explore their dedicated profile to easily track where and when they are performing. Whether you're discovering new artists or following familiar favorites, Groupie Tracker brings their concert information together in one place.
